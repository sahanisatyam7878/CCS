History of Computer

The history of computers is a fascinating journey of human innovation and technological advancement. It spans several centuries, from early calculating devices to modern-day supercomputers.

1. Early Calculating Devices (Pre-20th Century)

Abacus was the earliest calculating device used around 3000 BC. Later, Pascal developed Pascaline (1642) and Leibniz created Stepped Reckoner (1673).

2. Mechanical Computers

Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine. Ada Lovelace became the first programmer.

3. Electronic Computers

  • ENIAC (1945)
  • EDVAC (1949)
  • UNIVAC (1951)

4. Generations of Computer

  • 1st Gen – Vacuum Tubes
  • 2nd Gen – Transistors
  • 3rd Gen – IC
  • 4th Gen – Microprocessor
  • 5th Gen – AI
